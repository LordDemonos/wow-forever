The Horde route

Open the guide
  1. 1–13 Tirisfal. Bag, campfire, herbalism book, then the warlock wand.
  2. 14–20 Ragefire, Ruins of Lordaeron, then the sleeping bag. Camp Taurajo fills the gaps.
  3. 22–40 Shadowfang through Scarlet Monastery. Train staves before Shadowfang. Save 100 gold by Krol'dok.
  4. 41–60 Uldaman to Upper Blackrock Spire. Swap wands when the rare ones drop.
